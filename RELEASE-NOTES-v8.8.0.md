# KING Plus v8.8.0 — Ecosystem Pro

This stage expands the largest remaining parity areas with original KING Plus implementation.

- KTV Studio: realtime room queue/search, host/co-host shared singer stage, 90/180 second stage state, local microphone rehearsal recording and playback.
- PK Arena Pro: Red/Blue teams, realtime Firestore state, 90-second battle timer, gift-backed score display and live lead/result presentation.
- Family Pro: family level derived from members/activity/treasury points, append-only family treasury points, daily activity/check-in, share code, existing family chat, and Family voice call through the existing Jitsi media bridge.
- Rank Center: Level, VIP, Game, Chat and Event ranking tabs.
- Gift Universe: links secure wallet gifting, room gift wall, wish, lucky visual gift, collection/backpack; standalone Gift Center grows from 12 to 36 original gift entries with categories.
- KING Arcade: structured access to Online Ludo, board/skill, deduction, party and quick games plus realtime Party Room games.
- Party Room: new animated original KING stage backdrop with particles, spotlights, platform depth and per-theme palettes.
- Party Tools/More menu: adds Ecosystem Pro entry while preserving existing Parity Center.
- Family Firestore rules: adds validated member-only treasury point entries and family activity entries.
- No billing behavior is changed. TEST/free paths remain unchanged.
- No Bolo Hi proprietary source or assets are copied.

This is a major parity expansion, not a claim of exact third-party UI/resource duplication.
