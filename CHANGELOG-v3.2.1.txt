KING Plus v3.2.1 — Party Roles & Security

- Adds server-backed host/co-host room roles.
- Host can grant/revoke co-host.
- Host/co-host can lock/unlock seats, mute/unmute seats, remove seats, kick and room-ban members.
- Firestore enforces room lock and room bans; client-only checks are no longer authoritative.
- Adds private room flag plus per-user access grants for invites.
- Private room subcollections (members, seats, messages, events) require room access.
- Room password/secret is intentionally not stored in readable room metadata; secure password verification remains a separate server-side stage.
- Keeps no-billing mode and mobile test OTP 123456 unchanged.
