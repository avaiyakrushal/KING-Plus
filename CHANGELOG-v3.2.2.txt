KING Plus v3.2.2 — Party Password & Seat Requests

- Adds password-protected private room entry without storing the password/hash in readable room metadata.
- Password proof is checked by Firestore rules against a non-readable room secret; no paid Firebase Functions are required.
- Adds per-seat lock/unlock for host/co-host.
- Locked/global-locked seats now support guest seat requests.
- Host/co-host can view seat requests and approve or deny them.
- Moderator-approved users can be placed on the requested seat under Firestore authorization.
- Keeps v3.2.1 host/co-host, kick/ban, private access and moderation protections.
- Keeps no-billing mode and mobile test OTP 123456 unchanged.
