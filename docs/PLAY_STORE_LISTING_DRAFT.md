# KING Plus — Play Store Listing Draft

## App name
KING Plus

## Short description
Join live voice rooms, chat with friends, send gifts and discover communities.

## Full description
KING Plus is a social voice and community app built around live rooms, real-time conversation and social discovery.

With KING Plus you can:
- Join or create live social rooms.
- Chat with room members in real time.
- Follow people and keep up with your community.
- Send virtual gifts using server-managed coins.
- Receive room, message, follow and gift notifications.
- Use profile, wallet, safety and reporting controls.
- Access moderation tools when an account has approved admin privileges.

Recharge purchases, when enabled for production, are processed through Google Play Billing and verified by the KING Plus backend before coins are credited.

Voice features may use a third-party real-time communications provider. Final provider details must be reflected in the Privacy Policy and Data Safety form before public release.
