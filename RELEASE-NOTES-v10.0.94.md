# KING Plus v10.0.94 — Daily Check-in (TEST)

- Adds a permanent-account daily check-in claim backed by Firebase callable transactions. A claim is limited to once per Kolkata calendar day, safe on retries, and syncs streak and total activity XP across devices.
- Grants 10 nonredeemable Activity XP per confirmed check-in only; it does not change wallet Diamonds, VIP or money. Other task shortcuts remain navigation only until their activity verification is available.
- Keeps account check-in data readable only by its owner; direct client writes are denied.

This is a TEST build. The callable functions require deployment to the owner-controlled Firebase project before online check-in becomes active.
