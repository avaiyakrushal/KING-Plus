# KING Plus v10.0.59 — Recharge UI + zero-money interactive trials

Built from signed v10.0.58. Real money is NOT enabled. Both Diamonds and Crystal now show UPI/Agent/Card/Google Pay accordion sections. Sample tiers can be selected for a ₹0 DEMO across methods, with a confirmation screen, account-scoped local-only DEMO receipt history, and no real wallet/Beans/VIP/Crystal changes. A read-only Crystal placeholder remains until a real currency ledger exists.

The original signed-in, server-recorded Diamond Recharge History and Check Payment recovery remain. Real Cashfree new checkout and Continue Payment are explicitly blocked in this TEST build, even if a merchant catalog is unexpectedly enabled. Agent portal continues with its verified role restrictions, not monetary credit. Card/Google Pay do not collect personal or card details.

**Not completed here:** live paid UPI/Card/GPay/Agent/Crystal purchases or settlement. These require verified merchant keys/KYC, deployed secure backend, purchase/refund tests and store-compliant payment rules.
